# Code of conduct

English and 简体中文 technical contributions are welcome. Treat people respectfully
regardless of background, experience or communication style.

- Discuss evidence, code and tradeoffs; disagree constructively.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, impersonate or disclose personal data.
- Do not post credentials, subscriber data or private communications.
- Respect original copyright, attribution and others' existing work.
- Accept corrective feedback and proportionate moderation without retaliation.

These expectations apply to issues, PRs, reviews and other project-managed spaces.
The maintainer may remove inappropriate content or restrict participation to
protect the community.

For harassment or private-data exposure, use GitHub's **Report content / Report
abuse** tools; do not open a thread exposing the victim or reporter. Security
vulnerabilities follow the separate [security policy](SECURITY.md). Non-sensitive
process concerns may be raised in an issue without exposing private identities.
